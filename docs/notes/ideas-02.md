# Brainstorm — round two

Notes taken after the first complete run, start to finish, ark built. Kept verbatim as the
source for `docs/ROADMAP.md`.

---

1. Galoshes that allow wading in shallow water, reduced move speed though.
2. Resources can spawn at a slow rate as the days progress? Maybe. Not convinced but it's an idea.
3. Punished for working on the sabbath, but it could just be a gag?
4. Get rid of the road to the Ark, let's make it its own panel that is one panel higher than Noah's spawn. Plus let's make that whole panel not random. Big building platform in the center the bottom, stairs up to it, boat will fill the whole platform as it is built.
5. Hermit house should be enterable from the south, and at least a bit off the road. Dungeon door mechanics.
6. Too easy to get late-game items (serpent rod) early because you start there. We could barrier the entrance or (probably better) add internal dungeon barriers to prevent getting it early. Block it with pitch so it can only be collected later?
7. Seems like the road is a straight line from top to bottom! Let's try to keep the layout a but more interesting.
8. Also, let's make the gorge already connected but also dry. And make it impossible to walk on (can't get down into it).
9. I think the skinny dark horizontal lines are supposed to help indicate elevation? But they aren't really working for me. Find a different way.
10. Also it's weird that there are impossible ledges with stairs but then you can just walk around them? Maybe we add impassible ledges everywhere at round-numbered elevations, ex. there will always be a ledge between elevations 9 and 10, 19 and 20, etc. Not those numbers exactly but that's the idea. Then the whole world gets an elevation-map vibe too.
11. No impassable blocks in front of stairs.
12. Lower shrine keeps getting swallowed before I get there. Fine in principle, but we need an axe to enable progression in this case.
13. For pickaxe/axe, how about normally-harvestable blocks remove less durability and previously/unbreakable ones reduce more? Like a gopher tree might remove 1 and a regular tree might remove 2 or 3, and still yield 1 gopher wood. Then we tune the durability of the item.
14. The skiff should not be able to go into the deepest water. Also blocks you can't walk through (like rocks) at depth 2 or greater, you should be able to float over. Maybe once you have the skiff, you can upgrade it at higher docks? Then you could go to depth 2, or 3, or unlimited. And if you had the sounding line, you could see clearly to the bottom in the squares around the skiff? Something like that. Then you can dredge deeply/fish and see what you're doing.
15. Also, let's make the skiff and actual boat that you don't carry in your pocket. It stays in the world, you can lug it across the land but at half move speed or so. Then it deconflicts with the galoshes. Also it makes it possible to lose the boat! If it's sitting on the world and the deep water fills in around it, it will be inaccessible and you'll have to build a new one (good).
16. Currently on day 10, and I think the whole river should be flooded to a depth of 1 right now. It's like rain running down from the mountaintop.
17. The normal in game water should be too deep to wade, so like equivalent depth 2, but don't change how it looks!
18. Let's start getting more items into the game! Axe, pickaxe, map, galoshes, sounding line. Put them in dungeons to replace header container, in the hermit house, in a quick and dirty shop in town, etc. Make it clear which building is the shop.
