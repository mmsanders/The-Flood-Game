# Brainstorm — round one

Raw notes, kept verbatim as the source for `docs/ROADMAP.md`.

---

Some ideas and thoughts about the game so far:
1. Things seem to be working generally, but it's not that fun yet. But I think the foundation has a lot of potential!
2. I think there aren't enough reasons to explore, and the map feels too random. The randomness kind of works for me in the woods, but other than that not so much. In Noah's ark, the world is flooded because of PEOPLE, so where is everyone? Where is civilization?
3. In that vein, I think the world should be more coherent. I'm open to what that means, but perhaps: more structure for what appears in each biome. Maybe one town or city per, with roads that sort of connect them. More structure around the shrines, like they are places people actually go (maybe an NPC could say something like "we worship south-east of town to give a clue). They could appear in towns/cities or outside but fairly close with well-traveled paths. Sheep could be in someone's pasture on a farm outside of town. Etc. Find a reason to put things in places.
4. Also, I think some more high-level geographic features could be cool. Maybe there's a big gorge/river that runs from the top to the bottom and floods early, but allows the player to traverse the main world and some tributaries by skiff. Maybe there's some higher mountains or plateaus with bridges between them. Maybe the dungeon entrances actually appear in a block of rocks, like it's a hole in the wall, or in a deep grove of trees. 
5. I think it would be a fun Easter egg if the east and/or west world barrier in the far south was a desert. It's sandy near that entrance, and if you walk in, you get a note with something like "Endless sand stretches in every direction..." Then any way you walk except for back onto the map is just a repeat of the same tile. An NPC somewhere can give you a randomized 8 digit N/S/E/W pattern to reach a secret location or shrine or desert in the dungeon.
6. The snake rod is way too long looking now! It just needs to touch the second tile, it doesn't need to go all the way through it.
7. I think there should be a shop in each biome where you can trade later-game resources for earlier game ones that have flooded. I lost my last round because I was a few wood short and couldn't get more. They should be expensive though, like 5 rock for 1 wood, etc. Because those people have plenty of rocks but no wood. Supply and demand, you know.
8. I also think there should be more places to build boats. Maybe in town there's a carpenter shop, or every sufficiently-large body of eater has a skiff platform or something. And make sure there are a few large bodies of water at different biomes.
9. I wish I could see the verticality more. Going North doesn't feel enough like going "up" right now, it mostly just feels flat. Maybe some impassible ledges with stairs would help? Or just some artistic tricks on the tile designs? 
10. Upgrading the rod should be WAY more expensive.
11. More clues as to where things are located would be nice to make the exploration feel less random and more like playing detective.
12. I can't decide if the roguelike "quick runs" setup is right for the game. One of the best things about Zelda 1 for me is having an opportunity to explore, and the flood pace is messing with that via instant urgency. Maybe the player could get a week to explore freely, and then an ominous "The rain begins to fall...", the art can change to be less sunny and more gloomy, water can start trickling down ledges (art only), etc. THEN the urgency kicks in once the player has had a chance to see the world a bit. But then it might get too easy to get all the resources... so we'll have to troubleshoot that.
13. Once dungeons are in, I think they should NOT be immune to the flood. I think time may have to move slower, but I think the water should "drain" into the dungeon, aka the dungeon could be accessible even if the surrounding tiles are underwater, but the rain will fall to the bottom floor of the dungeon and fill it upward. The player explores the dungeon generally top-to-bottom, and a flood hitting the dungeon while they are in it could make the bottom floor(s) inaccessible and eventually force the player to resurface.
14. I think the Ark should be a BIG building platform on a tile. Make it look special, you have to climb stairs to it, it dominates the whole panel. Then as you spend resources on it, it constructs a HUGE boat, say it will take up 70-80% of the whole panel when complete.
15. I think Noah should have a little tent where he spawns initially, like his camp or home. It doesn't have to be enterable, at least not yet.
16. I think collecting animals should be harder. Like, you have to spend fiber to put them on a rope, and then you have to actually walk them places. You can tie them down and leave them somewhere, but eventually you have to actually walk every animal all the way to the Ark for them to board.
17. I think there should be a couple more items in the game. Ex. a map. Until you have it, you can see the tiles you've explored but they are all just gray on the minimap. Once you get the map they color in. And, you should be able to press a button (say m) to open a fullscreen mini map to see the whole world, which would also be gray and say something like "A map would help" until you have the map, and then it has full color and detail of the panels that we've explored (except for collectable items) so the player can be reminded of where things are, the world is pretty big. Another item is a fishing rod, to pull up drowned resources that are below dredge depth. Another could be a compass that points to the Ark. I do think it would be cool to have these things also be nautical-related somehow, like they might have a function on the actual Ark as well. Open to suggestions. Items can be dungeon rewards.
18. Towns should be a few panels big, maybe 2ish main ones with a bit of sprawl into the surroundings. Cities could be more like 4? And have stone roads outside instead of dirt. I think the mountaintops should have no city or town, but maybe a few isolated individual dwellings. The rock biome can have a city. The forest can have a logging town type place, and the fields can have a big tent city like you hear about with the Israelites. I think the fields shrine should be in a "tent of meeting" type structure, the forest could be in a grove, the rock city could be in a more traditional church/cathedral, and the mountaintop can be like a big cairn or rock pile structure.
19. Heart containers shouldn't just be lying around. They can be in dungeons, or you can trade for them in some (not all) shops, or you could find a friendly hermit who will give you one in exchange for something like your resources or a particular animal or something.
20. Towns could sell items that help traverse the world. Ex. an axe that can remove some number of "non-destructable" trees, provide gopher wood, and break after 5-10 uses. Same for a pickaxe and rocks. This can help the player traverse the map when they want to get through a bit of panel maze without going all the way around. We don't have currency though so we'll have to find a way to trade for it, or make it appear in dungeons sometimes, etc.
